## 3. Deviations from the registered plan

1. **Embase was not searched.** Embase.com was reachable through the institutional route on 29 September 2026 and the Emtree check was done, but no search was run. On 4 October 2026 the institutional entitlement was no longer recognised and access could not be restored. [Add here if a librarian or a colleague later runs the search.]
2. **Google Scholar was not searched.** Google returned its unusual-traffic block page and no results were seen.
3. **Citation searching was not completed.** The lists of documents citing Ganarin et al. 2021 were viewed in Web of Science and in Scopus on 4 October 2026 (13 documents in each) but were not exported or screened. The reference lists of the previous reviews were not searched. [Checking the reference lists of included reports at the full-text stage: author to decide whether to keep this in the plan.]

What this does and does not show: all 47 English-language reports from the pre-registration pilot were retrieved by the PubMed search (47/47); the Web of Science and Scopus searches retrieved 40/47 and 43/47, and a title search showed that the missing reports are not indexed in those two subscriptions. The pilot is a hand-collected sample and is not an independent test of sensitivity. The effect of the omitted sources on the set of reports found has not been tested and will be stated as a limitation.

## 4. Unchanged

Review question, eligibility criteria, primary exposure (duration of the index symptomatic episode), secondary exposures, primary outcome, data extraction, risk-of-bias approach, synthesis plan and timetable (start 28 September 2026, completion 28 June 2027).
