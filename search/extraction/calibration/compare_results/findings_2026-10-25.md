## Points to settle in the discussion (all open; none is decided here)
1. Splenectomy with no explicit viability word (guide section 7, row "total splenectomy, viability not written": NR after re-reading the report). E2 coded Non-viable with basis Intraoperative in 14 patients where E1 coded NR. What the reports (WS1859 table, WS0668) actually say decides whether E2's reading or E1's is right; the guide does not tell how to treat an operative description such as necrosis or infarction that is given without the word viability.
2. "Not mentioned": the guide says NR (2.3, 5.2, 5.4). The pattern above shows one file using No where the other uses NR, and the reverse for `recurrence` and `further_surgery`, for which the guide has no No/NR rule; add one.

4. `idx_dur_lower_bound` (rule 9): the guide's examples contradict each other (3 days at admission, no operation time: Yes; 14 hours, "immediate operation", no time: not flagged). Define when a duration is a lower bound.

6. Imaging and `ws_basis`: the 17 `img_us` differences in WS1859 and the `ws_basis` differences follow from what the WS1859 table says about imaging; read the table once.
