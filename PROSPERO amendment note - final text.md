## 4. Screening procedure (for the field describing study selection; protocol Section 14)

Screening is done in Rayyan with blind mode on until both reviewers (Jun Shu, Fei Peng) have finished a stage; author and journal details are hidden. Before formal screening, the two reviewers independently screened a random calibration set of 50 of the 2,548 deduplicated records (seed 20261004): they agreed on 45 of 50 (90.0%; Cohen's kappa 0.83), and all five disagreements were among the 11 records without an abstract. Author and journal details were visible to both reviewers during part of the calibration. A written screening guide, revised after the calibration, applies the registered eligibility criteria without changing them: when in doubt, Maybe; every Exclude carries one of six fixed reasons; records without an abstract are rated Maybe or Exclude only; conference papers with an abstract only are excluded as not a formal article; theses with clinical data and mixed adult–child series are kept as Maybe for full-text assessment. Rayyan's AI functions, automatic duplicate detection and automatic duplicate resolution are not used. Where one reviewer excludes and the other includes or marks Maybe, the reviewers discuss the record and, if they do not agree, the adjudicator (Jun Yang) decides from an exported list outside Rayyan; all steps are recorded and the completed list is kept with the review files. Differences between Include and Maybe (both go to full text) are counted and reported but not adjudicated. Agreement at the title and abstract stage will be reported as Cohen's kappa, in addition to the kappa for data extraction already in the protocol. Formal screening has started and is not complete; the dates are given under additional information.
